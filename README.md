#### 🏢 AI Floor-Plan Generator *(Capstone)*
Upload a plot boundary and choose a BHK mix to get **three floor-plan concepts** with a
lift/stair core, exported as **AutoCAD DXF**, PNG and JSON.

- Architectural grammar + **exhaustive search** with rule-based scoring, with **no training data**
- Door, daylight and room-size checks drawn from building-design practice
- **Local LLM assistant** (Ollama) that answers questions from DCPR-2034 and cites pages
- 31 unit tests

`Python` `Flask` `Shapely` `OpenCV` `ezdxf` `Ollama` `RAG`
